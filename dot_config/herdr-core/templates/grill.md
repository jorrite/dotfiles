Interview me relentlessly about the plan or design below until we reach a shared understanding.
Treat it as a design tree: every decision branches into the decisions that depend on it.
Work in rounds. Each round, ask every question whose prerequisites are already settled,
numbered, each with your recommended answer worded so that "yes" accepts it.
Then wait for my answers before the next round.
Look up facts in the repo yourself; never ask me for something you can find. The decisions are mine.
Stop when no open branch is left. Do not change any files, and do not act on the result until I confirm.
